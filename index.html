A little about me

Language opens a world of connection.

I am a Hindi educator who brings care, clarity and years of classroom experience to every lesson.

With a Master’s degree in Hindi and a Bachelor of Education, I have taught as a guest lecturer at T. John College and Dayanand Sagar College in Bengaluru. I have also supported secondary and high school students through online and in-person tutoring, helping learners strengthen their skills and prepare with confidence.
